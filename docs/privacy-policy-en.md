# Score Sorter - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: May 2, 2026**

## Overview

Score Sorter ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App operates and what data practices we follow.

## Data Storage

**All data in Score Sorter is stored exclusively in your device's RAM (Random Access Memory).** This means:

- No data is saved to your device's persistent storage
- No data is written to your device's internal storage or SD card
- No database or file system storage is used
- All application data exists only while the App is running

## Data Persistence

- When you close the App, all data is immediately cleared from memory
- When you restart your device, all application data is deleted
- No data persists between app sessions
- Uninstalling the App removes all associated data

## Data Transmission

**We do not transmit any data to external servers or services.** Specifically:

- No data is sent to our servers
- No data is sent to third-party services
- No data is shared with advertisers or analytics providers
- The App does not require internet connectivity to function
- The App collects no information about your activities

## Personal Data Collection

**Score Sorter does not collect, store, or process any personal data**, including but not limited to:

- Your name or contact information
- Your location or device identifiers
- Your device usage patterns
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Device identifiers or tracking information
- Any other personally identifiable information

## Permissions

The App may request certain permissions from your device as needed for core functionality. Any permissions requested are used only for the App's operation and no data is collected or transmitted as a result.

## Security

Since all data is stored only in RAM and is not transmitted anywhere, your data is protected by:

- The isolation of your device's memory
- The absence of data transmission risks
- Immediate deletion when the App closes

## Children's Privacy

Score Sorter does not knowingly collect information from children. Since we collect no data whatsoever, this applies universally.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date above.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the app store listing or GitHub repository.
